Rewatchability Score™ is a standardized 40–99 scale that establishes a clear benchmark for identifying the most compelling games in sports – spoiler-free.
Currently live for the NBA and WNBA, the standard is actively scaling across major professional and collegiate sports, establishing a consistent benchmark across leagues and seasons.
Designed for modern streaming, media, and archival platforms, Rewatchability Score™ supports smarter game discovery, highlight prioritization, scalable curation, and enhanced rewatch experiences.
